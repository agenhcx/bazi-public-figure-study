# Validation Plan — Music × BaZi, 1990–1992 (North-primary) — V1

**Freeze this file before inspecting 1990–1992 Music × BaZi association results.**

## Confirmatory sample

- Gregorian birth years: **1990, 1991, 1992**, pooled.
- Primary hemisphere rule: **birth latitude > 0** using Wikidata P19 → P625.
- Missing birthplace coordinates are excluded from the primary sample.
- Southern-Hemisphere births are excluded from the primary sample.
- Dates with a within-civil-date year/month-pillar transition are excluded.

## Outcome

Binary **Music** classification from the existing frozen Wikidata P106 broad-category mapping.

At the analysis level, use grouped-binomial daily counts:
- successes = Music people born on a date
- failures = non-Music people born on the same date

## Primary hypothesis

### H1 — 月令伤官 positive

`month_order_tengod == 伤官` is positively associated with Music occupation.

Primary model:

`Music/non-Music counts ~ H1_feature + C(gregorian_year) + C(gregorian_month) + C(weekday)`

- grouped-binomial GLM
- HC0 robust covariance
- one-sided positive coefficient test

H1 is the sole primary hypothesis.

## Secondary hypotheses

### S1 — overall Water lower

Among Music-classified north-primary people, total Water contribution is lower than matched-calendar expectation using the six-component Y/M/D main-qi representation:

1. year stem
2. year-branch main qi
3. month stem
4. month-branch main qi
5. day stem
6. day-branch main qi

The null is generated by matched-calendar Monte Carlo sampling within each Gregorian birth year, drawing whole date-level six-component vectors.

- Direction: lower Water.
- Test: one-sided lower-tail Monte Carlo p-value.

### S2 — 月令劫财 negative

`month_order_tengod == 劫财` is negatively associated with Music occupation.

Same pooled grouped-binomial GLM and covariates as H1.

- Direction: negative.
- Test: one-sided negative coefficient test.

### S3 — 月令比肩 positive

`month_order_tengod == 比肩` is positively associated with Music occupation.

Same pooled grouped-binomial GLM and covariates as H1.

- Direction: positive.
- Test: one-sided positive coefficient test.

## Multiplicity

- H1: sole primary test, no adjustment against secondary hypotheses.
- S1–S3: adjust jointly using **Benjamini–Hochberg FDR across exactly 3 secondary tests**.

## Stopping rule

- Analyze the pooled **1990–1992** confirmatory cohort.
- Do not stop, revise hypotheses, or change directions after inspecting 1990 or 1991 separately.
- Per-year estimates are descriptive only.

## Sensitivity analyses

After the frozen north-primary analysis:
- `known` sample may be analyzed as sensitivity.
- `full` sample may be analyzed as sensitivity.

Sensitivity analyses do not replace the primary north-only result.

## Interpretation rule

- A direction observed in 1986–1989 is not considered replicated unless supported in the frozen 1990–1992 validation analysis.
- Failure to reproduce the direction counts as non-replication.
- No new 1990–1992-derived hypothesis may be described as confirmatory in this validation.
