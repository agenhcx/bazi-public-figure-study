# Chess 1994/2013 Post-Reveal Closeout v2

## Status

The 1994/2013 chess cohorts are now hypothesis-generation data for any pattern
not already frozen before their first BaZi reveal.

No newly noticed pattern may be promoted to a confirmatory finding in those
same cohorts.

## Main results already observed

### Selection / Day Master

The preregistered Metal-earlier / Earth-later temporal hypothesis failed.
The observed 1994 -> 2013 direction was reversed.

Overall five-element Day-Master frequencies were broadly balanced.

### Selection / Shangguan and Pianyin

Pianyin showed no convincing enrichment.

A nominal Shangguan exposure enrichment appeared in 2013 male players.
Post-reveal diagnostics showed this was mainly compatible with fewer SG=0
players and more SG=1 players, not a strong SG>=2 upper-tail enrichment.

Having at least one Shangguan OR Pianyin is intrinsically common under the
same-birth-year calendar null (~two thirds), so individual examples have low
evidential value without baseline comparison.

### Performance / Shangguan and Pianyin

Neither six-position Shangguan count nor Pianyin count showed a positive
relationship with within-snapshot-sex standardized Elo.

### Performance / Day Master

A post-reveal Day-Master performance diagnostic found a small pooled
association with Elo-z:

- five elements: small omnibus association;
- ten stems: small omnibus association.

The effect sizes were small.

A particularly coherent directional pattern was:

- Metal mean Elo-z > Earth mean Elo-z in all four 1994M/1994F/2013M/2013F
  strata;
- Geng (庚) mean Elo-z > Wu (戊) mean Elo-z in all four strata.

These are post-reveal findings and require prospective validation.

## Post-reveal ten-Day-Master selection pattern

The pooled ten-stem frequencies suggested the following frozen direction:

- 乙 > 甲
- 丙 > 丁
- 戊 > 己
- 辛 > 庚
- 壬 > 癸

Equivalently, predicted stems are:

    {乙, 丙, 戊, 辛, 壬}

and opposite stems are:

    {甲, 丁, 己, 庚, 癸}

This pattern was discovered after viewing the 1994/2013 data and is therefore
exploratory until tested prospectively.
