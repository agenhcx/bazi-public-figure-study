## 2. Main post-validation observations

### A. Earth-Day-Master matching-main-qi tomb-month structure
Operational definition:
- 戊日主 + 辰月
- 戊日主 + 戌月
- 己日主 + 丑月
- 己日主 + 未月

This is a Day-Stem × Month-Branch relational structure, not a claim that Earth Day Masters are generally enriched.

M2 model:
`Music/non-Music ~ feature + C(gregorian_year) + C(gregorian_month) + C(weekday) + C(day_stem) + C(month_branch)`

Observed estimates:
- 1986–1989: OR ≈ **1.1669**
- 1990–1992: OR ≈ **1.3353**
- 1986–1992 pooled exploratory: OR ≈ **1.2390**

Exact-combination direction diagnostic:
- 1986–1989: **3/4** exact combinations had OR > 1.
- 1990–1992: **4/4** exact combinations had OR > 1.
- 1986–1992 pooled: **4/4** exact combinations had OR > 1.

Classical 建禄 and strict 阳刃 did not show the same pattern.

## 3. Exploratory branches that did not explain the signal

### 杂气月透干
Within 戊辰 / 戊戌 / 己丑 / 己未, marginalizing the unknown hour over 12 representative double-hours did not yield a stable transparency mechanism.

### Creator-coded musicians
Creator-coded P106 labels:
- singer-songwriter
- songwriter
- composer
- record producer
- music producer
- music arranger

Within Music, creator-coded vs other Music-coded people showed approximately null association with month-order 伤官:
- 1986–1989: OR ≈ **1.0158**
- 1990–1992: OR ≈ **1.0172**
- pooled: OR ≈ **1.0206**

Non-Fire month-order 伤官 was also not enriched among creator-coded Music people.

This branch is closed as an explanatory mechanism under the current P106-based creator definition.
