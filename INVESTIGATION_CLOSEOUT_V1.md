# BaZi Occupation Study Investigation Closeout V1

Date: 2026-09-29

## Scope
This closeout covers the existing Music, Table Tennis, and Chess datasets and all BaZi hypotheses examined in the current investigation.

## Hypothesis-generation freeze
No additional BaZi features, subgroups, cutoffs, transformations, or substantive hypotheses will be generated from the existing Music, Table Tennis, or Chess datasets after this point.

Future work on the existing datasets is restricted to evidence audit, reproducibility and provenance checks, manuscript preparation, figures and tables, and clearly labeled sensitivity analyses that do not introduce new substantive BaZi hypotheses.

Any genuinely new BaZi hypothesis must be evaluated using a new independent dataset under a new frozen or preregistered analysis plan.

## Final evidence position
Most exploratory occupational patterns did not reproduce in independent or prospective validation.
The clearest current positive finding is the prospective replication of six-position Shangguan enrichment in male elite chess; this is a group-level selection association, not evidence of individual-level prediction or stronger Elo performance.
Elemental concentration did not show a robust cross-domain association with elite selection or stronger performance.
The final birth-year analysis found no cross-domain monotonic trend toward greater elemental concentration in later-born players. Table tennis showed a weak player-level calendar-adjusted trend, but it disappeared when birth years were weighted equally; chess showed no corresponding trend.
