1. Do not calculate BaZi during discovery.
2. First construct the chess population and data-quality report.
3. Freeze inclusion rules, performance endpoints, time windows, and hypotheses.

- exact Gregorian DOB
- gender
- nationality/federation
- FIDE ID where available
- title (GM/IM/WGM etc.)
- peak classical Elo
- year of peak Elo
- title-achievement year if recoverable
