\## Locked holdout hypotheses



\### H1 — Earth vs Metal Day Masters



Professional table-tennis players show enrichment of Earth Day Masters

relative to Metal Day Masters.



Training observation:

Earth = 90

Metal = 69



This hypothesis was generated post hoc and requires independent replication.



\### H2 — Earth Day Master root enrichment



Among Earth Day Masters, Earth-main-qi branches

辰 / 戌 / 丑 / 未 are enriched relative to a calendar null conditioned on:



\- Gregorian birth year

\- exact Day Master stem



Training observation:

observed Earth-branch share = 41.11%

conditional-null expectation = 36.11%



Interpretation to test:

Earth-root structure may be related to selection into elite table tennis.



\### H3 — Metal selection / performance threshold



Metal Day Masters are less common in the professional-player sample,

but selected Metal Day Masters show stronger average competitive performance.



This direction was observed using both:



\- gender-standardized dynamic peak Elo

\- official career-best world ranking



The pattern is especially strong for 庚 Day Masters.



Interpretation:

Metal may have a more stringent selection threshold.



This is a selection hypothesis, not an established causal conclusion.



\### H4 — 辛 Day Master partial-seal requirement



Among 辛 Day Masters, 偏印-type Earth branches

丑 / 未 are enriched relative to a birth-year + 辛-day conditional calendar null.



Candidate interpretation:

辛 Day Masters may require stronger 偏印 structure to cross the professional

table-tennis selection threshold.



Training-set performance correlations:



偏印 count vs peak Elo z:

n = 25

Spearman rho = +0.3042

one-sided permutation p = 0.07184



偏印 count vs official peak rank:

n = 26

Spearman rho = -0.2973

one-sided permutation p = 0.06930



These are directionally consistent but underpowered and not conventionally

significant.



\### H5 — Earth roots as selection rather than performance gradient



Among already-professional Earth Day Masters, additional Earth roots do not

strongly predict competitive performance.



Training results:



Earth-root count vs peak Elo z:

n = 75

rho = +0.08695

p = 0.22817



Earth-root count vs official peak rank:

n = 79

rho = -0.06876

p = 0.27466



Candidate interpretation:

Earth roots may matter more for crossing the selection threshold than for

performance variation after selection.



\## Important negative results



Year-stem 食神 showed an exploratory frequency enrichment, but it was

cohort-driven and not robust to multiplicity.



食神 did not predict stronger dynamic Elo.



Age-12 食神 / 七杀 Dayun exposure was not enriched versus the

calendar-matched null.



These negative results remain part of the frozen training record.



\## Holdout rule



A holdout dataset must:



1\. contain no player from the current 384-person training cohort;

2\. be selected using criteria fixed before calculating BaZi features;

3\. use the same BaZi conversion conventions;

4\. use the same hypothesis definitions and expected directions;

5\. report all H1–H5 results regardless of statistical significance.
