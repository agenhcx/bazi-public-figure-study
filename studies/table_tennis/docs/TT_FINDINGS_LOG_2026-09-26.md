# Table-Tennis BaZi Study — Findings Log
Date: 2026-09-26

## Scope and evidence status

This document freezes the interpretation of the table-tennis work before moving to a new sport.
The 1985–2008 cohort (N=384) was the hypothesis-generation/training cohort.
The 1950–1984 historical cohort (N=335) was frozen before BaZi reveal and used as an independent holdout.
Performance rules for the historical cohort were also frozen before reveal.

Important: results discovered only after the historical reveal are explicitly marked exploratory and must not be used to redefine the frozen H1–H5 family.

---

## A. Training-set findings (1985–2008, N=384)

### A1. Year-stem 食神 frequency
- Observed: 53/384 = 13.80%
- Calendar-matched expectation: ~9.99%
- Enrichment: ~1.38x
- One-sided p ≈ 0.0104
- Two-sided p ≈ 0.0207
- Holm across 10 Ten-God categories ≈ 0.207
- Strongly influenced by the 丙子 cohort.
- Removing 丙子: 45/360 = 12.5%, one-sided enrichment p ≈ 0.0709.

Training interpretation:
Weak exploratory enrichment, cohort-driven and not multiplicity-robust.

### A2. 食神 and competitive performance
Dynamic Elo:
- Food God n=50 vs non-Food-God n=270
- Common-language probability ≈ 0.455
- One-sided p ≈ 0.842
- Top-tail tests also showed no positive Food-God effect.

Training interpretation:
No evidence that Food God predicts stronger table-tennis performance.

### A3. Age-12 Dayun 食神/七杀
Primary chronological-age-12 stem union:
- Observed ≈ 19.67%
- Calendar null ≈ 20.03%
- One-sided enrichment p ≈ 0.566

Training interpretation:
No support for childhood Food-God/Seven-Killings Dayun enrichment.

### A4. Day-Master composition
Counts:
- 木 75
- 火 75
- 土 90
- 金 69
- 水 75

Earth vs Metal:
- 土 90 vs 金 69
- Calendar-adjusted Earth–Metal signed difference was only borderline/exploratory.

### A5. Earth-Day-Master roots
Among 戊/己 Day Masters:
- Earth-main-qi branch share ≈ 41.11%
- Conditional calendar null ≈ 36.11%
- One-sided p ≈ 0.0275
- Two-sided p ≈ 0.0511
- 劫财 component was the strongest exploratory cell.

Training interpretation:
Candidate selection/root signal, but post-hoc and not a clean confirmed effect.

### A6. Metal performance
Within the professional cohort:
- Metal had descriptively stronger dynamic Elo than other elements.
- Metal > non-Metal one-sided post-hoc p ≈ 0.0322
- Metal > Earth one-sided post-hoc p ≈ 0.0258
- Global 5-element Kruskal p ≈ 0.242

Training interpretation:
Exploratory “selection threshold” hypothesis, not a global element effect.

### A7. 辛 partial-seal hypothesis
Among 辛 Day Masters:
- 丑/未 (己 main qi, 偏印-type exposure) showed an exploratory conditional-null enrichment.
- Within-pro performance correlations were directionally favorable but underpowered:
  rho ≈ +0.30, one-sided p ≈ 0.07.

- Calendar null = 9.989%
- Enrichment ratio = 1.016

## D. Post-hoc temporal Day-Master finding

### D1. Historical-only Earth–Metal birth-year pattern
Historical cohort:
- 土 n=59, mean birth year ≈ 1974.20, median 1976
- 金 n=79, mean birth year ≈ 1972.54, median 1974

Metal share among Earth+Metal:
- 1950s: 63.6%
- 1960s: 65.6%
- 1970s: 57.6%
- 1980–84: 47.2%

Trend tests:
- Mann–Whitney one-sided p ≈ 0.0966
- Logistic OR per 10 years later ≈ 0.785
- Calendar-adjusted one-sided p ≈ 0.129

Interpretation:
Directional but not significant in the historical cohort alone.

### D2. Pooled 1950–2008 exploratory analysis
Combined N = 719.

Overall five-element counts are approximately balanced:
- 木 152
- 火 130
- 土 149
- 金 148
- 水 140

Global clustering:
- 5-element × 5-year-bin Monte Carlo: not significant
- 10-stem × 5-year-bin Monte Carlo: not significant

Specific temporal pattern:
- Metal Day Masters are concentrated earlier.
- Earth Day Masters are concentrated later.
- Pooled Earth–Metal calendar-adjusted trend is nominally significant (approximately z=-2.74, two-sided p≈0.006).
- Element-wise trend: Earth later (raw p≈0.0155), Metal earlier (raw p≈0.059); Earth does not survive Holm across five elements.
- Exact-stem trend is mainly suggestive for 己 later and 庚 earlier; exact-stem family correction is not significant.

Interpretation:
This is an exploratory H6 generated after the historical reveal, not a confirmation of H1.

### H6 (exploratory, generated post-reveal)
Among elite/pro table-tennis players, Metal Day Masters may be relatively concentrated in earlier birth cohorts and Earth Day Masters in later birth cohorts.

This must be tested in an independent population.

## E. Candidate temporal-regime mechanism

A speculative candidate mechanism was proposed after observing H6:

- 七运 / 兑金: 1984–2003
- 八运 / 艮土: 2004–2023
- 九运 / 离火: 2024–2043

Possible prediction:
- environments associated with different periods may alter which Day-Master elements are selected into elite populations.

This is theory only. It must be made falsifiable before testing.
Do not use post-hoc interpretive flexibility such as switching between “土生金” and “土重金埋” after seeing results.

A future independent study should lock:
1. whether exposure is defined by birth year or elite-entry/success year;
2. exact period boundaries;
3. directional element predictions;
4. one primary statistic before BaZi reveal.

## F. What did NOT survive replication

1. Year-stem Food-God frequency enrichment.
2. Food God as a stronger-performance marker.
3. Age-12 Food-God/Seven-Killings Dayun enrichment.
4. Training-set Earth>Metal composition as a stable cross-era population property.

These negative replications are part of the final evidence and should not be omitted.

## G. Next independent project

Recommended next domain: international chess.

Reasons:
- very large historical elite population;
- strong quantitative performance measure (Elo);
- comparatively weak direct body-size / strength selection;
- long time span;
- suitable for both selection-frequency and within-elite performance tests;
- independent enough from table tennis to test H6 and other frozen structures.

Important limitation:
Exact birth time is rarely available, so formal “格局” labels such as 伤官格 / 偏印格 should not be the primary variable unless birth-time completeness is demonstrated.
For date-only samples, pre-register observable three-pillar proxies instead:
- month stem Ten God;
- month-branch main-qi Ten God;
- count/exposure of 伤官 or 偏印 in known three pillars;
- Day-Master element;
- Earth-root / Metal temporal H6;
- performance outcomes such as peak Elo / title attainment age.

Only after the chess cohort and hypotheses are frozen should BaZi variables be calculated.
