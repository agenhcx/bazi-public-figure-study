TT TRAINING DI-WANG / YANG-REN VS AGE-AT-PEAK SCREENING V1
==================================================================================

ROLE: post-hoc outcome screening in TT training only.
Historical holdout is not read.

Training N=384
Complete approx_age_at_peak N=335
Observed peak-age range=14.75 to 38.50

OUTCOME
-------
Frozen approx_age_at_peak = approximate age when the player's recorded
career-best world ranking was reached.
This is NOT age at first world ranking / first Top-100 / first Top-50.

PRIMARY TEST
------------
Age-at-peak is centered within exact birth-year x gender strata.
Exposure labels are permuted within those same strata while preserving
the observed exposed count in each stratum.
Negative effect = exposed players peaked earlier.

INTERPRETATION GUARDRAILS
-------------------------
1. This is screening/post-hoc, not confirmatory.
2. YEAR_DIWANG overlaps prior Year-Branch Jie-Cai for 8/10 Day Stems.
3. The primary permutation controls exact birth year and gender because
   younger cohorts have less follow-up and a mechanically younger peak-to-date.
4. approx_age_at_peak is not a debut/first-ranking milestone.
5. If there is a strong earlier-peak signal here, the next data collection
   target should be age at first Top-100 / Top-50 / Top-20.
6. Historical TT holdout remains untouched.
