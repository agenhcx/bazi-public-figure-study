## Public targeted review

- **17/17 leads reviewed at source discovery level**. 10/17 now have at least one external institutional, scholarly, official-Academy or contemporary original-news explicit day-level birth-date statement; they are **source-checked claims**, not 10 accepted study DOBs. One more (Pierre Rouard) has a *derived third-party civil/death index* date and remains without independent original verification. Six lack checked external DOB sources in this pilot.
- **Jean Dieudonné (CTHS 112083): 1906-07-01, born Lille.** Academy's own published archival fonds inventory (PDF pages 4-5, printed page labels 4-5) explicitly states correspondent 1965 -> member 1968. This is the **only person in these 17 for whom THIS pilot verified an original French Academy document explicitly recording full-member transition**. The original CTHS date-claim audit field was empty. URL: https://www.academie-sciences.fr/pdf/dossiers/fonds_pdf/11J_Jean_Dieudonne.pdf .
- **Charles Louis Dubost (CTHS 112087): 1914-10-01**, heart surgeon, published in contemporary 1968 Le Monde. Do **not** conflate him with **Charles Dubost (born 1905)**, jurist/prosecutor at the Nuremberg tribunal. The 1941 surgical dissertation original cover is web-indexed with 1914-10-01 but its full scan could not be fetched in this review. URL: https://www.lemonde.fr/archives/article/1968/05/15/l-etat-du-malade-qui-a-subi-a-l-hopital-broussais-une-transplantation-cardiaque-est-tres-satisfaisant_2503590_1819218.html .
- **Georges Millot (CTHS 101487): 1917-05-24.** Société géologique de France and contemporary Le Monde 1977 both state May 24. *Persée's bibliographic page TITLE* reads `27 mai 1917`, whereas the historical article's **printed headline and body** both say `24 mai 1917`. This is a **metadata-text discrepancy**, not two verified conflicting birth acts; retain it in a provenance note rather than silently ignoring it. https://www.persee.fr/doc/sgeol_0302-2692_1992_num_45_1_1878 .
- **Alexandre Favre** is recorded by Marseille's own Academy as born 1911-02-23, elected to **Marseille** on 1967-01-05, and Paris Academy member 1977. Those are elections to **different institutions**, never treat 1967 as a Paris membership year. https://www.academie-sla-marseille.fr/medias/files/succession-fauteuils-acadmarseille-5-.pdf .
- **Jean Piveteau**: the Royal Academy of Belgium confirms DOB 1899-09-23 and a **Belgian** associate election in 1961, not a French election. Contemporary Le Monde reports membership of the French Academy since 1956, but a French first-party record is still needed.
- **Paul Mandel**: Université de Strasbourg and a professional 1992 obituary independently state DOB 1908-11-14. Born Lodz (1908): original Polish/Russian historical civil-record calendrical convention remains unverified; no conversion performed.

## QA and non-promotion conditions

1. `source_specific_dob_evidence_checked=1` means an external public source explicitly states a date. It is **not** a finding of independent genealogical proof, Gregorian conversion, or entry into the analysis cohort; source independence from CTHS must be assessed carefully.
2. `french_academy_full_firstparty_checked=1` is given **only to Jean Dieudonné** because a French Academy archival document states his full-member transition. Other membership claims may be plausible and even externally corroborated, but the actual original 1989 directory pages have not been opened.
3. A status/election year from Belgian or Marseille academies MUST NEVER be imported as a Paris Academy membership event.
4. CTHS and Wikidata may share biographical sources. Agreement in DOB values is not independent confirmation without issuer-level evidence.
5. The 1835-1985 birth-year support and full-member-only primary study criteria remain unchanged, and 2018/2021 recent election-year gaps remain unresolved. No BaZi features are calculated before global freeze.
6. The data file contains ONLY compact authored evidence statements and source links, not copied CTHS full biographies or copyrighted scanned pages.
